Simulation controls

Move the slider to imitate a changing MQ-135 analog output. This is a logic demonstration, not a real air-quality measurement.

Threshold: 600
At 600 or above, the red LED and fan-control indicator turn on. Below 480, the green LED is on. Below 700, the yellow LED is on.

Indicative wiring plan

MQ-135 AO Arduino A0 (in simulation, a slider replaces the sensor)
LCD SDA Arduino A4 on Uno
LCD SCL Arduino A5 on Uno
Green LED D3 through a current-limiting resistor to GND
Red LED D4 through a current-limiting resistor to GND
Fan driver D8 → suitable base resistor → TIP120; use a separate fan supply and flyback diode

How the demo behaves

The simulated sensor value is below the demonstration threshold. Green status is on and the fan output is off.

Solar panel, battery charging, actual MQ-135 sensing, fan airflow and filter efficiency are not physically simulated on this webpage. Use a charge controller matched to the exact battery chemistry and voltage; never connect a solar panel directly to a rechargeable battery.
